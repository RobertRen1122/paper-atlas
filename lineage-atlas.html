Lineage

What each repo is, and the order that makes them cheap to read.

Wan2.1 is the foundation: an open suite of dense video diffusion transformers (T2V at 1.3B and 14B, CLIP-conditioned I2V, first/last-frame FLF2V, and the VACE editing stack) built on a causal 3D VAE (4×8×8, 16 latent channels), a UMT5-XXL text encoder, and flow-matching samplers. Everything downstream is a variation of its wan/modules/model.py.

Wan2.2 keeps the block architecture byte-for-byte compatible but changes how capacity is spent: the A14B models load two full 14B experts and switch between them at a timestep boundary during sampling; I2V drops CLIP entirely; timestep modulation becomes per-token; and a new dense TI2V-5B runs on a 2× more compressive VAE (4×16×16, 48 channels). Distribution shifts from xDiT/xfuser context-parallel to a home-grown Ulysses sequence-parallel. S2V and Animate arrive as sub-packages.

LingBot-World v2 (LingBot-World 2.0 / "Infinity", Robbyant; paper arXiv:2607.07534) forks the Wan2.2 I2V-A14B stack and turns it into an interactive world model: a chunk-causal transformer with a rolling self-attention KV cache (model_causal.py), a Self-Forcing-style distilled 4-step student for real-time 60 fps streaming (model_fast.py), and per-pixel Plücker camera/action conditioning injected by AdaLN into every block (cam_utils.py). Notably, it abandons the two-expert switching it inherited — one causal model does everything.

Recommended reading order. Master Wan2.1's core once — wan/text2video.py then wan/modules/model.py (about 900 lines total); that is the entire mental model. Then read Wan2.2 as a diff (experts, per-token timesteps, vae2_2, TI2V path), and LingBot as a diff on top of that (KV-cache attention, chunk loop, camera AdaLN, distilled sampling). The walkthroughs below are structured exactly this way.

The core generation pipeline

Wan2.1 foundation trace

An annotated run of WanT2V.generate — task t2v-14B, size 480*832, 81 frames, UniPC, 50 steps.

The size string 480*832 maps to (W, H) = (480, 832) — a portrait clip, and the only resolution class the 1.3B model supports. All token math is orientation-symmetric, so the landscape 832*480 gives identical counts. The config wan/configs/wan_t2v_14B.py:17-29 pins every constant used below: vae_stride=(4,8,8), patch_size=(1,2,2), dim=5120, ffn_dim=13824, num_heads=40, num_layers=40; shared config adds text_len=512 and 1000 train timesteps (wan/configs/shared_config.py:9-18).

latent  T′ = (81−1)/4 + 1 = 21   H′ = 832/8 = 104   W′ = 480/8 = 60
tokens  per frame: (104/2) × (60/2) = 52 × 30 = 1 560
        seq_len: 21 × 1 560 = 32 760 (720p: 21 × (90/2)(160/2) = 21 × 3 600 = 75 600)
  1. CLI entry & defaults

    generate.py resolves task defaults: 50 sampling steps for T2V (40 for I2V), shift 5.0 (3.0 for I2V at 480p, 16 for FLF2V/VACE), guidance 5.0, solver unipc, then builds WanT2V from WAN_CONFIGS.

    generate.py:70-85 · wan/configs/__init__.py:20-28

  2. T5 text encode[L≤512, 4096] ×2

    UMT5-XXL, encoder-only: 24 layers, d=4096, 64 heads, vocab 256,384, bf16. The prompt and the negative prompt are each tokenized to at most 512 tokens and encoded; the pipeline keeps the unpadded embeddings (the model pads to 512 later).

    wan/text2video.py:174-184 · wan/modules/t5.py:456-468

  3. Latent shape & seq_len[16, 21, 104, 60]

    target_shape divides frames by the VAE's temporal stride 4 (with the +1 for the initial frame) and pixels by spatial stride 8; seq_len further divides H′·W′ by the 2×2 spatial patch — the arithmetic in the box above, computed exactly here.

    wan/text2video.py:159-166

  4. Noise init[16, 21, 104, 60] fp32

    A single seeded Gaussian latent is drawn at full video length — the whole clip is denoised jointly, there is no autoregression anywhere in Wan2.1/2.2.

    wan/text2video.py:186-195

  5. Solver setup50 timesteps

    FlowUniPCMultistepScheduler.set_timesteps(50, shift=5) builds a flow-matching σ grid on [0,1] and warps it by σ′ = 5σ/(1+4σ), pushing steps toward high noise; timesteps are 1000·σ′. A dpm++ variant (FlowDPMSolverMultistepScheduler) is selectable.

    wan/text2video.py:206-225 · wan/utils/fm_solvers_unipc.py:162-211

  6. Denoise loop + CFG2 fwd / step × 50

    Each step runs the DiT twice — once with the prompt context, once with the negative context — and combines uncond + 5.0·(cond−uncond) before the scheduler step. Steps 7–11 below are inside one such forward.

    wan/text2video.py:233-246

  7. Patchify → tokens[1, 32760, 5120]

    patch_embedding is a Conv3d(16→5120, kernel=stride=(1,2,2)): [16,21,104,60] → [5120,21,52,30], flattened and transposed to a token sequence; grid_sizes=(21,52,30) is kept for RoPE and unpatchify, and sequences are zero-padded up to seq_len.

    wan/modules/model.py:456-457 · wan/modules/model.py:534-543

  8. Time & text embeddingse [1,5120] · e₀ [1,6,5120]

    The scalar timestep gets a 256-d sinusoid → MLP → e; a second projection expands it to six modulation vectors e₀. Text embeddings are zero-padded to 512 tokens and mapped 4096→5120 by a 2-layer MLP.

    wan/modules/model.py:546-559

  9. Block: AdaLN self-attnq,k,v [1,32760,40,128]

    Per block, a learned (1,6,5120) modulation table is added to e₀ and chunked into shift/scale/gate triples. Self-attention: LayerNorm (no affine) → scale-shift → Q,K,V with RMSNorm on Q,K → 3D RoPE → FlashAttention (2 or 3), full bidirectional over all 32,760 tokens — no windowing (window_size=(-1,-1)) — then a gated residual.

    wan/modules/model.py:296-306 · wan/modules/model.py:105-159 · wan/modules/attention.py:24-130

  10. 3D RoPE detailhead 128 = 44+42+42

    Rotary frequencies are precomputed for 1024 positions and split per 128-d head into 44 dims for time, 42 for height, 42 for width; each token's rotation is the outer product of its (f,h,w) grid coordinates. Text tokens get no positional encoding.

    wan/modules/model.py:480-485 · wan/modules/model.py:42-70

  11. Block: cross-attn + FFNK,V from 512 text tok

    Cross-attention (with an affine LayerNorm, cross_attn_norm=True) reads the projected text tokens; then the FFN (5120→13824→5120, tanh-GELU) runs under its own scale-shift-gate. T2V uses WanT2VCrossAttention; I2V swaps in a two-stream variant (below).

    wan/modules/model.py:308-316 · wan/modules/model.py:162-184

  12. Head → unpatchify[16, 21, 104, 60]

    A final LayerNorm with a 2-gate AdaLN feeds a linear 5120→64 (= 1·2·2 patch × 16 channels); unpatchify einsums tokens back onto the latent grid. The output is the flow velocity v-prediction at this timestep.

    wan/modules/model.py:320-347 · wan/modules/model.py:584-607

  13. Scheduler stepx_t → x_{t−1}

    UniPC's multistep update advances the latent; after the 50th step the loop's output is the clean latent x₀.

    wan/text2video.py:248-254

  14. VAE decode[3, 81, 832, 480]

    The Wan2.1 VAE (encoder dim 96, z=16, temporal downsampling in the last two of three stages) decodes latent frames one at a time through causal Conv3d layers with a 2-frame feature cache, upsampling 2× temporally in two of its three stages and 8× spatially; output is clamped to [−1,1] and written at 16 fps.

    wan/text2video.py:261 · wan/modules/vae.py:544-568 · wan/modules/vae.py:592-604

1.3B numbers. Same architecture at dim=1536, ffn_dim=8960, 30 layers, 12 heads — head_dim stays 128, so the RoPE split is unchanged (wan/configs/wan_t2v_1_3B.py:20-29). Block parameters ≈ 46M vs 352M, i.e. ≈1.4B vs ≈14.1B in the trunk. 480p only.

image2video.py — CLIP-conditioned I2V

I2V keeps the trace above and adds two conditioning paths. Path 1, semantic: the reference image goes through CLIP ViT-H/14 (224px, patch 14 → 257 visual tokens of width 1280, wan/modules/clip.py:471-478), is projected by MLPProj into DiT width, and is prepended to the text context (wan/modules/model.py:350-369, wan/modules/model.py:561-563); WanI2VCrossAttention attends to the image tokens with dedicated k_img/v_img projections and adds that result to the text attention (wan/modules/model.py:187-229). Path 2, pixel-exact: the image is VAE-encoded as frame 0 of an otherwise-zero clip, and a 4-channel binary mask marking "given" frames is stacked on top, giving y 20 channels (wan/image2video.py:210-217, wan/image2video.py:240-249); y is concatenated with the 16-channel noisy latent, so the I2V DiT has in_dim=36 (wan/modules/model.py:530-531). Resolution is derived from max_area and the image aspect ratio (wan/image2video.py:183-197); defaults are 40 steps, shift 3.0 at 480p.

FLF2V and VACE in one paragraph each

FLF2V is I2V with two anchors: the mask marks the first and last frame (msk[:,1:-1]=0, wan/first_last_frame2video.py:231-238), the VAE encodes [first, zeros…, last] (wan/first_last_frame2video.py:262-276), and CLIP encodes both frames — 2×257 context tokens distinguished by a learned position embedding in MLPProj (wan/first_last_frame2video.py:257-258, wan/modules/model.py:359-367). Default shift jumps to 16.

VACE (all-in-one editing, 1.3B and 14B) subclasses the T2V model with a parallel stack of "vace blocks" on every second layer (vace_layers = 0,2,4,…, wan/modules/vace_model.py:90-96). A 96-channel packed context (masked video, masks, reference images) is patchified separately, run through the parallel blocks, and injected into the main trunk as additive hints scaled by context_scale (wan/modules/vace_model.py:136-152, wan/modules/vace_model.py:58-61). The pipeline lives in wan/vace.py:37.

Distribution, 2.1-style. Multi-GPU sampling patches the model with xDiT/xfuser "USP" forwards — Ulysses all-to-all inside ring attention — configured by --ulysses_size × --ring_size (wan/distributed/xdit_context_parallel.py:93-181, generate.py:292-305). Remember this — it is one of the things Wan2.2 rips out.

Wan2.2 delta from 2.1

Same block, new capacity plan. Read this section as a diff.

Identical: the DiT block anatomy (self-attn with RMS-normed Q/K and 3D RoPE, affine-normed text cross-attn, tanh-GELU FFN, six AdaLN gates), the 44/42/42 RoPE split, UMT5-XXL with text_len=512, the UniPC/DPM++ flow-matching solvers, patch (1,2,2), and — for the A14B models — the exact 14B dimensions (5120/13824/40/40) and the Wan2.1 VAE, re-shipped verbatim as wan/modules/vae2_1.py (byte-identical to 2.1's vae.py up to the class rename). Changed: five things.

1 · Two experts, switched by timestep

Each A14B pipeline loads two complete 14B DiTs from subfolders high_noise_model and low_noise_model (wan/text2video.py:101-117). Every denoise step picks one: if t ≥ boundary × 1000 the high-noise expert runs, else the low-noise expert (wan/text2video.py:169-201, wan/text2video.py:306, wan/text2video.py:341-352). Boundaries come from configs — T2V 0.875, I2V 0.900 — along with per-phase CFG scales: T2V (low 3.0, high 4.0), I2V (3.5, 3.5), 40 steps, shift 12.0 for T2V / 5.0 for I2V (wan/configs/wan_t2v_A14B.py:30-37, wan/configs/wan_i2v_A14B.py:30-37). The idle expert is offloaded to CPU. It is coarse-grained diffusion MoE: 2×14B parameters on disk, 14B active per forward.

2 · CLIP is gone

Wan2.2's model.py deletes WanI2VCrossAttention and MLPProj; a single WanCrossAttention reads text only (wan/modules/model.py:158-181). I2V-A14B conditions purely through the pixel-exact path — mask + VAE-encoded first frame, y 20ch, in_dim=36 (wan/image2video.py:289-296, wan/image2video.py:314-323) — with no image encoder in the loop, and otherwise runs the same trace as 2.1's I2V plus expert switching.

3 · Per-token timesteps

The forward now accepts t of shape [B] or [B, L]: it is expanded per token, so e becomes [B, L, 5120] and e₀ [B, L, 6, 5120], and every block modulates each token with its own timestep (wan/modules/model.py:460-468, wan/modules/model.py:232-259). This is the hook TI2V uses to mark conditioning tokens as already-clean — and it is exactly the machinery LingBot inherits for its causal cache-commit pass.

4 · TI2V-5B and the 2.2 VAE

The dense 5B model (dim 3072, ffn 14336, 24 heads, 30 layers, wan/configs/wan_ti2v_5B.py:16-36) does both T2V and I2V in one checkpoint at 704×1280, 121 frames, 24 fps — a single 14B-free path meant for one consumer GPU. It runs on the new Wan2_2_VAE: pixels are first patchified 2×2 in pixel space (3→12 channels), then encoded with two temporal downsamples to a stride-(4,16,16), 48-channel latent (encoder dim 160, decoder 256; wan/modules/vae2_2.py:783-790, wan/modules/vae2_2.py:888-899, wan/modules/vae2_2.py:1014-1022).

vae2_1: 4·8·8 = 256 px → 16 ch (48× compression) · vae2_2: 4·16·16 = 1 024 px → 48 ch (64×)
TI2V-5B tokens: T′=(121−1)/4+1=31, grid 704/16=44 × 1280/16=80 → 31 × (44/2)(80/2) = 31 × 880 = 27 280

The TI2V I2V path is where per-token timesteps pay off: the image is VAE-encoded and written into latent frame 0; masks_like zeroes frame 0 of the mask, the latent is re-composited as (1−m)·z + m·noise after every solver step, and the timestep vector sent to the model is 0 on frame-0 tokens and t elsewhere (wan/textimage2video.py:548-551, wan/textimage2video.py:567-598, wan/utils/utils.py:172-200). No mask channels, no CLIP — the image is just a clean region of the latent.

5 · Distributed rewrite

xfuser/xDiT is dropped entirely. Wan2.2 ships its own Ulysses attention — all-to-all that scatters heads and gathers sequence (wan/distributed/ulysses.py:26-47) — under a sequence-parallel DiT forward that chunks tokens (and the per-token e) across ranks and all-gathers at the end (wan/distributed/sequence_parallel.py:64-145). Ring attention and the --ring_size flag no longer exist; parallelism is --ulysses_size only.

S2V and Animate, briefly

S2V-14B (speech-to-video) wraps the 14B trunk with a wav2vec2 audio encoder whose features are compressed by a CausalAudioEncoder and injected by per-layer audio cross-attention at 12 of the 40 blocks (audio_inject_layers, wan/configs/wan_s2v_14B.py:38-40, wan/modules/s2v/model_s2v.py:352-364); long clips are generated segment-by-segment with FramePack-compressed motion frames as context and zero timesteps on reference/motion tokens — the one Wan2.2 task that generates autoregressively.

Animate-14B (character animation/replacement) adds a second Conv3d that patchifies skeleton-pose latents and adds them onto the video tokens after patch embedding, plus a face branch: face crops go through a motion encoder and a FaceAdapter fuses the resulting motion vectors by cross-attention at every 5th block; CLIP returns for reference-image features, and a relighting LoRA ships for the replacement mode (wan/modules/animate/model_animate.py:292-343, wan/modules/animate/model_animate.py:365-367, wan/configs/wan_animate_14B.py:13-15). Neither is traced further here.

LingBot-World v2 delta from 2.2

The research payload: Wan2.2's I2V stack rebuilt as a causal, camera-driven, real-time world model.

The repo is a stripped fork of the Wan2.2 I2V-A14B stack — one task, one config (wan/configs/wan_i2v_A14B.py:18-37), the same 14B dimensions, vae2_1, t5, and the Ulysses distributed layer carried over. Around that trunk it adds four files that change what the model is: model_causal.py, model_fast.py, cam_utils.py, and a rewritten image2video.py that generates chunk-by-chunk against a KV cache. The paper (arXiv:2607.07534, "Infinite Worlds with Versatile Interactions") names the recipe: causal pretraining with a MoBA mask (Mixture of Bidirectional and Autoregressive attention), Plücker-embedding camera control injected by AdaLN, chunk-wise prompts under a causal cross-attention mask, then post-training that compresses the teacher with consistency distillation plus DMD run over long self-rollouts (Self-Forcing style) to get the 4-step real-time student.

model.py — the bidirectional base, diffed against Wan2.2

LingBot's wan/modules/model.py is Wan2.2's file with exactly one addition: camera conditioning. A control_type flag selects 6 Plücker channels ('cam') or 7 with WASD actions ('act') (wan/modules/model.py:394-398); a dedicated patch embedding patch_embedding_wancamctrl — Linear(6·64·1·2·2 = 1536 → 5120) — plus a residual 2-layer MLP embeds the folded Plücker volume (wan/modules/model.py:402-405, wan/modules/model.py:506-527); and every block gains four linears (cam_injector_layer1/2, cam_scale_layer, cam_shift_layer) that turn the per-token camera feature into an extra AdaLN applied right after the self-attention residual: x = (1+scale)·x + shift (wan/modules/model.py:220-224, wan/modules/model.py:255-263). Everything else — cross-attention, per-token timesteps, head — is unchanged Wan2.2. This bidirectional variant is not what the released pipeline instantiates (the README lists it as TODO); it documents the base the causal models descend from.

model_causal.py — causality by cache, not by mask

  • No attention mask anywhere. Causality is structural: a forward only ever processes the current chunk's tokens; everything earlier exists as cached K/V. Attention runs dense over [cache window + current chunk] (wan/modules/model_causal.py:187-189) — so frames within a chunk see each other bidirectionally, and chunks see only the past. The forward's docstring cites CausVid, Algorithm 2 (arXiv 2412.07772) (wan/modules/model_causal.py:582-586).
  • Globally-consistent RoPE. rope_apply takes a start_frame offset — the chunk's absolute frame index, derived from current_start // frame_seqlen — so temporal rotary phases keep advancing across an unbounded rollout (wan/modules/model_causal.py:35-63, wan/modules/model_causal.py:154-158).
  • Rolling KV cache with attention sinks. Per layer the cache holds K/V of shape [B, kv_size, heads, 128] plus global_end_index/local_end_index counters. When local_attn_size (a window in latent frames) is set and new tokens would overflow, the oldest tokens are evicted by shifting the buffer left — but the first sink_size frames are pinned in place, StreamingLLM-style, anchoring the rollout (wan/modules/model_causal.py:159-185). Queries then attend to at most max_attention_size trailing tokens (wan/modules/model_causal.py:187-192).
  • Cross-attention computed once. Text K/V are built on the first forward and cached for the whole session (is_init), since the prompt is fixed between user interventions (wan/modules/model_causal.py:244-253).
  • Same camera AdaLN as model.py, per block, after the self-attention residual (wan/modules/model_causal.py:322-325, wan/modules/model_causal.py:370-376), and per-token timestep modulation inherited from Wan2.2 (wan/modules/model_causal.py:642-651). Caches are passed per block index (wan/modules/model_causal.py:694-702).

model_fast.py — the distilled real-time student

Architecturally WanModelFast is WanModelCausal — same cam layers, same cache mechanics, same head; its file header credits Self-Forcing for borrowed functions (wan/modules/model_fast.py:1) and it re-imports the shared norms/attention from model.py (wan/modules/model_fast.py:10-17). The real differences are (1) weights — it loads the distilled checkpoint (fast_checkpoint='transformers' vs causal_checkpoint='transformer_causal') — and (2) latency engineering: a fast cache path with pure-Python indices that avoids every GPU→CPU .item() sync when the cache is global (wan/modules/model_fast.py:124-133), and frame_seqlen / cross_attn_first_call passed down as Python values so neither RoPE offsets nor the cross-attn init gate ever synchronize (wan/modules/model_fast.py:170-199, wan/modules/model_fast.py:485-498). The few-step sampling itself lives in the pipeline (trace below): 4 fixed timesteps, x₀-prediction, re-noise, no CFG — this is the variant behind the 720p/60fps claim, distilled with consistency + DMD per the paper.

cam_utils.py — how an action becomes a tensor

  • Input: poses.npy — per-frame camera-to-world 4×4 matrices (OpenCV convention) — and intrinsics.npy [fx fy cx cy], which are rescaled to the working resolution (wan/utils/cam_utils.py:132-163).
  • Resample to latent time: poses are interpolated to one per latent frame — Slerp on rotations (with quaternion sign fixing), linear on translations (wan/utils/cam_utils.py:7-40).
  • Relativize & normalize: poses become frame-to-frame relative SE(3) transforms (framewise=True), translations scaled so the largest step has unit norm — the code comments cite CameraCtrl II for the normalization (wan/utils/cam_utils.py:56-75).
  • Plücker embedding: for every pixel, the ray origin and unit direction in the relative camera frame → a 6-vector; the full map is [f, H, W, 6] at pixel resolution (wan/utils/cam_utils.py:95-129).
  • Fold to latent grid: the pipeline rearranges each 8×8 pixel block into channels — 6·8·8 = 384 channels on the latent grid, [1, 384, lat_f, 60, 104] — chunked in step with the latent (wan/image2video.py:578-586); the model then patchifies 2×2 more (→1536-d per token), embeds to 5120, and injects per block as the AdaLN described above (wan/modules/model_causal.py:663-681, wan/modules/model_causal.py:515-519).
  • Discrete actions: keyboard-style WASD vectors can ride along as a 7th control channel (control_type='act'); example folders ship wasd_action.npy/ijkl_action.npy, and the fast pipeline has the concat path wired but disabled by default (wan/image2video.py:587-597).

  1. Session setupt ≈ [999, 968, 909, 769]

    WanI2VCausal loads WanModelFast with the window/sink sizes baked into every attention layer; a 1000-point UniPC schedule at shift 10 is built once and indices [0, 250, 500, 750] select the 4 sampling timesteps.

    wan/image2video.py:125-138 · wan/image2video.py:393 · wan/image2video.py:529-531

  2. Conditioning prepy [20, 88, 60, 104]

    Exactly Wan2.2 I2V: first frame VAE-encoded into an otherwise-zero clip, 4-channel frame-0 mask stacked on top. The T5 embedding is looked up in a sha256-keyed cache so interactive re-prompts skip the encoder; camera poses are loaded, truncated to the frame budget, and turned into the folded Plücker volume [1, 384, 88, 60, 104].

    wan/image2video.py:478-482 · wan/image2video.py:537-549 · wan/image2video.py:563-586 · wan/image2video.py:599-608

  3. KV cache allocation40 × [1, 28080, 5, 128]

    Per layer: zeroed K and V of kv_size = frame_seqlen × local_attn_size tokens (heads divided by the Ulysses world size — 40/8 = 5 here) with end-index counters, plus a per-layer text-K/V cross cache of 512 tokens.

    wan/image2video.py:616-635 · wan/image2video.py:992-1020

  4. Chunk loop22 chunks × 6240 tok

    Noise, conditioning y, and the Plücker volume are split into 4-frame chunks; each iteration passes current_start = chunk_id·4·1560 so attention and RoPE know the chunk's absolute position.

    wan/image2video.py:644-668

  5. 4-step denoise, no CFGx [16, 4, 60, 104]

    For each of the 4 timesteps: one forward predicts flow; _convert_flow_pred_to_x0 recovers x₀ = x_t − σ_t·v; if steps remain, x₀ is re-noised to the next timestep with fresh Gaussian noise and the loop repeats — consistency-style sampling, single stream, no negative prompt.

    wan/image2video.py:673-700 · wan/image2video.py:363-383

  6. Commit to cache1 fwd at t = 0

    The finished chunk is run through the model once more with timestep 0; that forward's K/V land in the rolling cache and become the context every later chunk attends to. If the cache is full, eviction shifts out the oldest non-sink frames first.

    wan/image2video.py:704-709 · wan/modules/model_causal.py:163-185

  7. Decode[3, 353, 480, 832]

    Chunk latents are concatenated and decoded once at the end in this offline script; the deployed system instead streams VAE decoding per chunk on dedicated workers (paper §4.1) — the causal VAE's frame-by-frame feature cache is what makes that possible.

    wan/image2video.py:711-718

  8. Pretrain mode (teacher)40 steps + CFG / chunk

    causal_pretrain swaps in WanModelCausal and does full 40-step UniPC with CFG 5.0 per chunk — which needs two KV caches (cond and uncond streams), a scheduler reset every chunk, and two t=0 commit passes. Same cache mechanics, ~20× the compute: this is the distillation teacher.

Where the fork diverges from its own config. The inherited A14B two-expert design is gone: one causal model serves all timesteps, and the config's boundary = 0.947 and sample_steps = 70 are read into the pipeline but never used (wan/image2video.py:100 is boundary's only consumer). Sequence parallelism also gets a causal variant that threads cache offsets through the Ulysses all-to-all (wan/distributed/sequence_parallel.py:249, wan/distributed/sequence_parallel.py:414), and a prewarm() hook pre-runs one dummy forward to move kernel autotuning and NCCL handshakes out of the first interactive chunk (wan/image2video.py:175-214).

Paper ↔ code map. MoBA mask and chunk-wise-prompt cross-attention masks are training-time constructs (§3.2) — the released inference code realizes their effect through the chunk loop and the once-cached text K/V. Consistency distillation + DMD over self-rollouts (§3.3) produced the model_fast weights. "Dynamic KV cache management" (§4.3.2) corresponds to the local_attn/sink/max_attention_size machinery. The agentic Director–Pilot harness and the spatio-temporal refiner are deployment-side and not in this repo.

Method & provenance

Every dimension, stride, boundary, and default on this page was read from the config files (wan/configs/*.py) and pipeline code of local checkouts pinned to the three commits in the header; every file:line link was verified against those checkouts before linking, and resolves to the same commit on GitHub. Sequence lengths are computed, not quoted: frames → latent frames via the VAE temporal stride, pixels → latent grid via the spatial stride, ÷ the 2×2 patch → tokens, with the arithmetic shown inline. The two derived step-split counts (≈26/14 and ≈14/26) apply the config shift to the scheduler's σ-warp formula and are marked as approximations. LingBot technique names (MoBA, consistency distillation, DMD, Plücker/AdaLN) follow the repo's paper, arXiv:2607.07534; the causal inference algorithm follows the CausVid reference cited in the code, and model_fast.py credits the Self-Forcing codebase.

One caveat for readers cross-checking checkpoints: in_dim=36 for I2V-style models is set by each checkpoint's config.json (via from_pretrained), not by the repo configs — the code paths cited above show where the 36 channels are assembled.
